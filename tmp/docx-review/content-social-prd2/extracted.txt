[BodyText] Requirement language:
[Paragraph] Must is mandatory in the stated phase and testable.
[Paragraph] May is permitted but not required.
[Paragraph] Derived means calculated from governed records rather than edited directly.
[Paragraph] All IDs are stable. A deferred requirement keeps its ID when promoted to a later release.
[Heading2] 3. Product decision
[Paragraph] Brand Circuit needs one controlled workspace to plan, produce, approve, publish and review content for:
[Paragraph] OG companies, including DELabs; and
[Paragraph] external clients managed by Brand Circuit.
[Paragraph] One client may own multiple brands. Brand Circuit controls staff assignment, workflows, approval configuration and operating standards. A separate Customer Platform will later expose appropriate requests, feedback and approvals to clients; it is not part of this module.
[Heading3] 3.1 Product hierarchy
[Paragraph] Brand Circuit workspace â†’ Client/company â†’ Brand â†’ Campaign/plan â†’ Content item â†’ Channel variant/publish job
[BodyText] All records, searches, dashboards, exports, credentials and audit events must preserve this hierarchy. Cross-client access is denied by default.
[Heading3] 3.2 Product outcomes
[Paragraph] Make every content item, owner, deadline, approval and next action visible.
[Paragraph] Reduce missed deadlines, lost feedback, unapproved publishing and duplicated work.
[Paragraph] Give management evidence-based staff and team performance visibility without invasive surveillance.
[Paragraph] Create a clean operating record that can later power publishing, inbox, listening and analytics integrations.
[Paragraph] Keep Launch cheap: staff time is also a cost, and no nice-to-have enters Launch.
[Heading3] 3.3 Initial operating benchmark
[Paragraph] The system must support configurable service-scope targets. The initial DELabs/Brand Circuit benchmark is:
[Paragraph] 14 publish-ready short videos per week;
[Paragraph] 5 publish-ready static or carousel assets per week; and
[Paragraph] 1 batch-production cycle per week.
[Paragraph] This is a seeded target template, not a hard-coded global quota. Targets vary by client, brand, period and role.

[Heading3] 12.1 Launch budget gate

[Heading3] 12.2 Cost hierarchy
[Paragraph] Native capability already available in COS.
[Paragraph] Existing fixed-price organisation subscription connected/shared through COS.

[Heading3] 12.3 Scale promotion gate
[Paragraph] A Scale requirement may start only when its decision record identifies:
[Paragraph] operational or commercial trigger;
[Paragraph] affected clients/brands/users;
[Paragraph] staff/build effort and cash cost;
[Paragraph] cheapest adequate manual/free/already-paid alternative;
[Paragraph] expected measurable benefit;
[Paragraph] security/data impact; and
[Paragraph] approving owner.
[Heading3] 12.4 Native replacement gate
[Paragraph] A third-party tool is replaced only when:
[Paragraph] the capabilities Brand Circuit actually requires are ready;
[Paragraph] native is cheaper or strategically superior;
[Paragraph] historical data can be migrated safely;
[Paragraph] security and performance tests pass;
[Paragraph] old and new run in parallel before switch; and
[Paragraph] rollback remains available.
